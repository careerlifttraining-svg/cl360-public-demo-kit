30-day employer pilot

Week 1 · Discovery

Observe actual dispatch board, paper tickets, material pickup and delivery-signoff process; map roles and exceptions.

Week 2 · Configure

Define company-specific dispatch fields, materials catalog, authorization roles, site acceptance and data handling.

Week 3 · Limited field test

Pilot with a dispatcher and a small driver group in a properly hosted, secured multi-user version.

Week 4 · Evaluate

Measure dispatch preparation time, missing paperwork, delivery exceptions, time to retrieve proof and driver feedback.

Commercial proposal outline

Offer a paid workflow assessment and fixed-scope pilot. Price only after validating operational volume, number of drivers, software integrations, security needs, support obligations and measurable savings.

Before any live customer data: implement authenticated access, per-company permissions, cloud sync, database backups, device security, proper electronic proof-of-delivery rules, retention policy and production QA.
